What happens to immunity in space?

Microgravity, radiation, and stress disrupt the immune system — often before symptoms appear

Viruses
Studies of ISS crews found herpesvirus DNA, including EBV and VZV, in samples collected during flight.
Inflammation
Chronic low‑grade inflammation rises — the immune system stays in "alert mode," draining resources.
Neuroendocrine Strain
Elevated cortisol and catecholamine levels severely suppress T-cell activity and disrupt natural circadian immunity during flight.
